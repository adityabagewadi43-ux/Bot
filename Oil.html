📈 What Drives Oil Prices?

Oil prices are primarily determined by global supply-demand dynamics, geopolitical events, OPEC+ production decisions, and inventory reports from the EIA and API. WTI crude trades on NYMEX while Brent trades on ICE, serving as global benchmarks.

Key factors include US shale production, Saudi spare capacity, China demand growth, and seasonal refinery maintenance schedules that impact crack spreads and refining margins.

🛢️ Advanced Oil Knowledge

WTI vs Brent Spread

The price differential typically ranges $2-8/bbl, reflecting US Gulf Coast logistics vs global benchmark dynamics. Widening spreads often signal US supply constraints.

Key Reports Schedule

  • Wed 10:30AM ET: API Weekly Inventory
  • Thu 10:30AM ET: EIA Weekly Petroleum Status
  • Fri Monthly: OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report

Trading Strategies

Crack Spreads: Long heating oil/gasoline vs short crude. Calendar Spreads: Near vs deferred months. Options: Straddles for inventory reports.

Risk Management

  • Position size max 2-3% account risk
  • Pre-define stop losses before entry
  • Avoid trading during API/EIA releases
  • Watch dollar index correlation
